SummaryAqua Kyoto Paris has no agreed GA4 booking target, so this report shows volume, month on month and year on year only. October recorded 762 GA4 bookings on the create path against 1,232 in September and 1,087 in October 2025, down 38.1 per cent on the month and 29.9 per cent on the year. All three figures are rebuilt on the create path so they sit on one basis; the published September monthly of 1,298 was on the older basis that also counted amendments. The October figure is not a trading result. The site booking tag broke on 16 October and had not recovered by month end, capturing about 0.43 of SevenRooms website bookings in the closing week against 1.20 in the matched week last year, and the restaurant did not serve on 24, 26, 27, 29 and 30 October. SevenRooms, which no site tagging touches, recorded 691 website bookings created in October against 846 in September and 740 in October 2025, and 1,469 bookings across all channels against 1,932 and 1,300, so website demand is down 6.6 per cent on the year while all channels are up 13.0 per cent. October spend closed at €7,373 on Meta against a €7,500 plan and €4,416 on Google against €6,500, so €11,789 of digital against a €14,000 plan. The Google shortfall of €2,084 is the outage that began on 22 October and ran to month end.
