Aqua USA — Covers & Booking Channels

26 Oct–1 Nov 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 19–25 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 27 Oct–2 Nov 2025
Both measures agree this week, which they have not done since the tag broke. GA4's booking event ran all seven days at all three properties, so the GA4 page and this page tell the same story rather than one understating the other. Covers created came in at 4,601, up 15.2 per cent week on week and up 12.8 per cent year on year, the highest point in the eight-week series. On the weekly email definition of Website plus OpenTable and Resy plus Google Reserve, covers were 2,670, up 27.3 per cent on the week and 10.3 per cent on the year. Website covers alone were 1,147, up 26.0 per cent and 6.4 per cent. All three venues are up on both measures. Read Hutong New York's OpenTable line with care. Its OpenTable and Resy covers went from 413 to 649, but every one of its 390 OT Guestcenter covers this week arrived inside four hours: 26 October at 15:00 (38 records), 29 October at 18:00 (36), 31 October at 16:00 (34) and 27 October at 14:00 (17), 125 records in total against 125 records for the whole week. OpenTable delivers to this venue in batches, so its week-on-week OpenTable movement is a sync pattern as much as demand. The mechanical rule only removes the past-dated part of those batches. Aqua NYC and Hutong Miami show no such concentration and their OpenTable lines are readable. The Hutong New York OpenTable past-dated re-sync has recurred for a seventh week running. The mechanical rule, which drops any booking whose service date falls before the Monday of the week it was created in, removed 66 records and 178 covers, all OT Guestcenter at Hutong New York, with service dates spanning 5 March to 25 October 2026 and creation times from 26 October 15:00 to 31 October 16:00. The single deepest hour, 26 October at 15:00, held 61 past-dated records and 167 covers. Nothing was removed at Aqua Roma, Aqua Kyoto NYC or Hutong Miami. This is the seventh consecutive week and it should be stopped at source. Google Reserve is down again and remains a margin question, not a demand one: at the two Hutongs, OpenTable and Resy covers are 854 against 522 a year ago while Google Reserve covers are 41 against 334. Both venues moved their Google Business Profile booking provider to OpenTable in March 2026. Aqua NYC is still on the direct SevenRooms integration and its Google Reserve covers are 229 against 146. Two movements at Aqua NYC are not demand and should not be read as such. Reception covers went from 217 to 498, and three bookings alone carry 182 covers with December service dates, so that is large group business being taken by the reservation team rather than a change in the week's trade; reception is not in bookable covers. Separately, Aqua Roma's Booking Widget covers went from 133 to 425 while its Dinner | à la carte - Landing Page tag fell from 122 to 20, which is a re-tagging on the venue's own site. Both tags sit in the website channel, so the website figure is unaffected and remains comparable. The recorded classification change was applied and it moved only the year-ago week. Flaming Duck NYC now reads as a partner rather than falling outside the channels. It appears nowhere in the reporting week or the recomputed prior week; in the year-ago week it puts 3 records and 13 covers at Hutong New York into the partner column, which is why last year's partner figure there is no longer zero. Neither column is in bookable covers, so the headline comparison is unchanged. One further boundary, recorded rather than hidden: single first names such as Mariana, Gaby and Damarys are now read as staff bookings, as Luz, Lucilly and andrew sicklin already were, rather than sitting outside the seven channels. That is a mechanical name-shape rule rather than a hand-written list, and it moves 7 covers out of the all-channel total in the recomputed prior week relative to last week's published basis. No USA venue shows the exclusive-hire signature in the reporting week or the matched 2025 week: dinner covers are above zero on every date at every venue in both years, so no service was lost and nothing is stripped from either side.
