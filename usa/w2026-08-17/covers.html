17–23 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 10–16 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 18–24 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it is blind to OpenTable and Resy, only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reservation desks, which together are the majority of US covers. That is why GA4 reads -29.8% YoY while total covers are 4,259 against 3,845 the same week last year. Third-party and walk-in demand is carrying the US venues while our own website channel weakens, which is the pattern worth acting on.
