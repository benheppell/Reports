Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website widget, so it is blind to OpenTable and Resy, only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reservation desks — together the majority of US covers. That is why GA4 reads -25% YoY while total covers hold up far better. Third-party and walk-in demand is carrying the US venues while our own website channel weakens, which is the pattern worth acting on.
