Aqua USA — Covers & Booking Channels

14–20 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 7–13 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 15–21 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. The USA’s GA4 bookings are down 44.8% YoY while covers are only down 10.2% YoY. Part of that difference is a routing change, not lost demand: both US Hutongs moved their Google Reserve provider to OpenTable in March, and at the two Hutongs OpenTable and Resy covers are up 15 percent year on year while Google Reserve covers are down 68 percent. That is a margin change to watch, not a demand collapse. Website covers are down 40 percent year on year across the market. Covers came in at 3,666, down 0.6 percent week on week. One import has been stripped out: 105 OpenTable records (273 covers) were loaded into Aqua Roma in a single hour on the evening of 14 September, every one for a date already past, so they are a backfill rather than new bookings. Left in, the market would read 3,939, up 6.8 percent week on week, and Aqua NYC’s OpenTable line would be 455 rather than 182. A smaller Hutong New York batch (138 covers, the same pattern as earlier weeks) is left in for consistency.
