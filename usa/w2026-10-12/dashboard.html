Read this week's GA4 booking numbers as broken, not as demand. The GA4 event sevenrooms_booking_complete stopped firing across all three USA properties part way through Thursday 15 October. Market create-path bookings by day were 74, 65, 64 and 50 on 12 to 15 October and then 2, 2 and 1 on 16, 17 and 18 October. Sessions on those three days were normal, SevenRooms recorded 125 website bookings and 378 website covers over the same three days, and Meta's Schedule conversion rose week on week at every venue, so the bookings happened and GA4 did not record them. The week's GA4 total of 258 therefore covers four days of tracking, not seven, and every GA4 figure on this page, including the week on week and year on year columns and the year to date, understates the market by roughly three days. The covers report is the usable read of the week. This is also the first USA week on the create-path booking definition. From the W37 run, GA4 bookings count only the create path and exclude the modify, manage and prearrival pages, where the same event fires when a guest amends a booking. The amendment share this week was 5.5 percent of all booking events across the market, 0 percent at Aqua NYC, 8.2 percent at Hutong NYC and 18.4 percent at Hutong Miami. In the year-ago week it was 16.7 percent at Aqua NYC and zero at both Hutongs, because in October 2025 the event still fired on the /reservations/<venue> landing route and no modify event existed there. The prior week, the year-ago week, the eight-week series and the year to date have all been rebuilt on the create path so both sides match, which means they do not tie to the figures published up to and including w2026-10-05.
