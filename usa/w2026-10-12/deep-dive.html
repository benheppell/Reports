  • Meta tells the opposite story to GA4 this week, which is the clearest evidence that the GA4 event broke rather than demand. Observations
  • Treat every GA4 booking figure for this week as incomplete. The event stopped firing part way through 15 October at Hutong NYC and after 15 October at Aqua NYC and Hutong Miami. Market create-path bookings by day were 74, 65, 64, 50, then 2, 2 and 1. SevenRooms recorded 125 website bookings on 16 to 18 October against 5 GA4 events, and sessions on those days were 80 to 90 percent of normal, so this is a measurement failure, not a demand collapse.
  • On the four tracked days the market booked 253, which annualises to a far better week than the 258 printed here. The figure is left as pulled rather than grossed up, because nothing in GA4 supports a filled-in number. Use the covers report for the week's demand and treat the conversion rates on this page as arithmetic on a broken numerator.
  • This is also the first USA week on the create-path definition, so the prior week, the year-ago week, the series and the year to date have all been rebuilt on the same basis and do not tie to earlier published figures. Aqua NYC is additionally restricted to its own venue paths, because in the year-ago week its property recorded 73 booking events on Hutong New York reservation pages that are already counted in the Hutong New York property. Read this week's bounce and engagement rates as provisional: they restate by 6 to 9 points in the week after a run.
