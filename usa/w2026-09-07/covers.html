Aqua USA — Covers & Booking Channels

7–13 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 31 Aug–6 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 8–14 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. The USA’s GA4 bookings are down 56.1% YoY while covers are only down 2.9% YoY. Most of that difference is a routing change, not lost demand: both US Hutongs moved their Google Reserve provider to OpenTable in March, and OpenTable and Resy now carry 25 percent of covers, up 52 percent year on year, while website covers are down 39 percent. That is a margin change to watch, not a demand collapse. Covers came in at 3,773, up 12.7 percent week on week. Treat that week-on-week rise with care: 181 covers at Hutong New York were entered in a single hour on 9 September, the latest in a run of backfill batches. They are left in for consistency with prior weeks, but stripped out the market is nearer +7 percent and Hutong New York’s OpenTable line falls from 552 to about 371.
