Aqua USA — Covers & Booking Channels

27 Jul–2 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 20–26 Jul 2026 · YoY vs 28 Jul–3 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. The USA mirrors London: GA4 website bookings read ▼12.7% YoY, yet total covers created are +38% YoY (5,881 vs 4,248). GA4's sevenrooms_booking_complete only fires for bookings made on our own website. It cannot see OpenTable + Resy — 1,300 covers this week, about a fifth of the total — only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reception desks. The YoY uplift is amplified by new Aqua Kyoto NYC (no prior-year base) and a strong reception/host booking surge at Aqua NYC, but the direction is clear: total covers are up week on week and year on year even as the website-widget channel softens. This report is the more accurate view of what we are actually creating.
