Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 bookings are 35.6% down YoY but covers are only 6.1% down, because GA4 only counts website-widget bookings and cannot see OpenTable or Resy (20 percent of covers), walk-ins or desk bookings (a further 41 percent). Covers came in at 3,437, up 2.2 percent week on week. Adjustment applied: 695 OpenTable records (1,942 covers) were loaded into Hutong New York in a 36-minute batch on 4 Sep with service dates running from 28 July to 19 October, and a comparable 999-cover batch sits in the year-ago week. Both have been removed here; leaving them in would have shown the market up 60 percent week on week, which is a data load rather than demand.
