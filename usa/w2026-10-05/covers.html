5–11 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 6–12 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. On the two venues with a usable last-year comparison, GA4 bookings are down 55.2% YoY while their covers are up 14.9%. Part of that difference is a routing change, not lost demand: both US Hutongs moved their Google Reserve provider to OpenTable in March, and at the two Hutongs OpenTable and Resy covers are up 37 percent year on year while Google Reserve covers are down 68 percent. That is a margin change to watch, not a demand collapse. Aqua NYC is still on the direct integration and its Google Reserve covers are up 7 percent year on year. Covers came in at 4,361, up 19.3 percent week on week and up 8.3 percent year on year, the highest of the eight weeks shown. On the weekly email definition of Website plus OpenTable and Resy plus Google Reserve, covers were 2,366, up 18.7 percent week on week and up 7.5 percent year on year. No bulk import has been stripped out of this week. Hutong New York had taken an OpenTable past-dated history re-sync in each of the four weeks to 28 September, so this was checked explicitly: the two largest OT Guestcenter bursts this week, 56 records on 5 October and 32 on 7 October, are 55 percent and 37 percent past-dated with service dates spanning only 30 September to 18 November, which is ordinary late and forward syncing. That four-week run has broken. The exclusions named in earlier weeks stay out of the comparison columns and the trend: the 40 records and 124 covers loaded into Hutong New York on 28 September stay out of the prior-week column and the W39 point, the two loads of 21 and 23 September stay out of W38, the 273-cover Aqua Roma backfill stays out of W37, and the 1,907-cover Hutong New York history load stays out of W35. The year-ago week carries no bulk import, so nothing is excluded there.
