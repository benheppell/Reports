Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website widget, so it is blind to OpenTable and Resy, only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reservation desks, which together are the majority of USA covers. That is why the two numbers diverge: GA4 reads ▼19.2% YoY on website bookings while total covers created across every channel are ▲10.2% YoY. OpenTable and Resy alone move close to 900 covers a week, entirely invisible to GA4. Read covers as the fuller demand and channel picture, not as a contradiction of the GA4 story.
