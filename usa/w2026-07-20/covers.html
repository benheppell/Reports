20–26 Jul 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 13–19 Jul 2026 · YoY vs 21–27 Jul 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. The USA mirrors London: GA4 website bookings read ▼17.7% YoY, yet total covers created are +11% YoY. GA4's sevenrooms_booking_complete only fires for bookings made on our own website. It cannot see OpenTable + Resy — 1,166 covers this week, about a quarter of the total — only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reception desks. So the GA4 decline reflects the website-widget channel, not demand: total covers are up week on week and year on year. This report is the more accurate view of what we are actually creating.
