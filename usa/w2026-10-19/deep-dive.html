Observations
  • Meta fell at every venue this week and that is a real move, not a tracking one: the Schedule conversion fires from the same confirmation page as the GA4 booking event, and it kept working through the dead days last week. Aqua NYC 69 to 59 schedules on $1,810, about $31 each; Hutong Miami 50 to 22 on $1,653, about $75 each and back outside the poor band after one good week; Hutong NYC 20 to 14 on $1,410, about $101 each and the worst of the eight weeks shown.
  • Aqua NYC spend split $976 prospecting for 32 schedules, $559 remarketing for 24 and $138 on the outdoor dining campaign for 3. The Halloween landing-page-view campaign took $137 and recorded no schedules, which is expected for that objective. Remarketing at about $23 a schedule is the cheapest line in the market.
  • Hutong NYC still parks $633 of its $1,410 in the Mahjong reach campaign and the video viewers campaign, neither of which is expected to book. Its conversions campaign alone ran at about $56 a schedule on $778, worse than the $41 it managed last week. Hutong Miami's retargeting campaign took $420 for 12 schedules at about $35 while its conversions campaign took $1,091 for 10 at about $109, so the whole deterioration there sits in prospecting.

Observations
  • The booking event is recording again. It was dead from 16 to 20 October at all three properties and came back on Wednesday 21 October. Market create-path bookings by day were 4 and 1 on 19 and 20 October, then 49, 54, 47, 60 and 46. So this week covers five days of the seven and the 261 printed here understates the market by roughly 110 bookings.
  • The recovery is evidenced three ways. SevenRooms took 86 website bookings on 19 and 20 October against 5 GA4 events, and from 21 October GA4 runs at or near its own pre-break ratio to SevenRooms website rows at every venue. page_view ran normally throughout, including the dead days, so collection never stopped. The USA properties carry no book_now_button_click event, so that cross-check is not available in this market. Hutong Miami is the one venue still sitting meaningfully below its own norm, 0.85 times against 1.22 to 1.29, a gap of about 13 bookings over five days, and it should be rechecked next week.
  • Bounce and engagement rates on this page are provisional and restate by 6 to 9 points in the week after a run. The prior week's session metrics have already restated: Aqua NYC sessions 7,070 to 7,227, average session duration 185.6 to 218.5 seconds and users 5,919 to 6,062; Hutong NYC sessions 5,529 to 6,101 and users 4,546 to 4,994; Hutong Miami sessions 4,456 to 4,600. Session duration is not inflated this week at any USA property, so the secondary symptom seen in Hong Kong and London has cleared here too.
