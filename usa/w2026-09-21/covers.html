21–27 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 14–20 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 22–28 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. On the two venues with a usable last-year comparison, GA4 bookings are down 61.7% YoY while covers are down only 1.0%. Part of that difference is a routing change, not lost demand: both US Hutongs moved their Google Reserve provider to OpenTable in March, and at the two Hutongs OpenTable and Resy covers are up 73 percent year on year while Google Reserve covers are down 57 percent. That is a margin change to watch, not a demand collapse. Covers came in at 4,044, up 12.9 percent week on week and up 0.4 percent year on year. Two imports have been stripped out: 76 OpenTable records (211 covers) loaded into Hutong New York in a single hour on the afternoon of 21 September, every one for a date in the week just gone, and a further 25 records (75 covers) on 23 September with service dates running back to March. Left in, the market would read 4,330 and Hutong New York's OpenTable line would be 937 rather than 651. A third, much smaller batch (5 records, 22 covers) is left in as ordinary late syncing. The 273-cover Aqua Roma backfill named last week stays out of the prior-week column and the W37 point, and a 1,907-cover Hutong New York OpenTable history load stays out of W35, as it did last week.
