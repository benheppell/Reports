Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

14–20 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 7–13 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 15–21 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong’s GA4 bookings are up 13.8% YoY and covers are up 49.4% YoY, so the two measures agree on direction, and covers show the fuller picture. GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 22 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (20 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 45 percent. Covers came in at 10,138, up 10.5 percent week on week. Reception and reservations-desk covers rose sharply at Aqua HK (167 from 28) and Hutong HK (281 from 98), which accounts for much of those two venues’ weekly gain. One caveat on the venue rows: the Shiro line combines Shiro Hong Kong and Shiro Tai Kwun, and Tai Kwun has no 2025 records at all, so its +83.5 percent year-on-year figure is two sites measured against one and is not like for like.
