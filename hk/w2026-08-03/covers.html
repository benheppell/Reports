3–9 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 27 Jul–2 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 4–10 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it never sees OpenRice (around a fifth of Hong Kong covers), only partly captures Google Reserve, and is blind to walk-ins and the reception desks, which together are close to half of all covers created. That is why the two numbers move differently: GA4 reads ▲13.3% YoY on website bookings while total covers created across every channel are ▲28.5% YoY. Read this as the fuller demand and channel picture, with OpenRice strength and heavy walk-in and reception volume at Dim Sum Library and Shiro, not as a contradiction of the GA4 story.
