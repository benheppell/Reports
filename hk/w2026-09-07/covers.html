7–13 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 31 Aug–6 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 8–14 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong’s GA4 bookings are up 18.1% YoY and covers are up 28.7% YoY, so the two measures agree on direction — covers simply show the fuller picture. GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 23 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (22 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 45 percent. Covers came in at 9,513, up 2.8 percent week on week. One caveat on the venue rows: the Shiro line combines Shiro Hong Kong and Shiro Tai Kwun, and Tai Kwun has no 2025 records at all, so its +103.9 percent year-on-year figure is two sites measured against one and is not like for like.
