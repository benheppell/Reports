Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

5–11 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 6–12 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Both measures moved the same way this week, and covers are the fuller read. GA4 bookings are down 1.9% YoY while covers are up 34.0% YoY, or up 11.1% on a like-for-like basis once Shiro Tai Kwun, which has no 2025 records, is taken out. GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 24 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (24 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 42 percent. Covers came in at 9,878, up 2.2 percent week on week, alongside GA4 bookings up 4.2 percent, so the two now point the same way. OpenRice had its strongest week of the eight at 2,414 covers and Google Reserve rose to 930 from 750, so both third-party routes grew together rather than trading against each other as they did last week. The largest single move in the table is a split, not a change in demand: Shiro Tai Kwun walk-ins rose to 1,023 from 533 while Shiro Pacific Place walk-ins fell to 447 from 946. Added together the two Shiro records booked 1,470 walk-in covers against 1,479 the week before, so the shift is in which record the walk-ins were entered against. Worth asking the Shiro team whether the desk changed how it logs them. Cantina rose to 933 covers from 741 with reception at 327 against 168, and Aqua HK reception rose to 119 from 13 on a 60-cover desk booking for 13 October. Dim Sum Library eased to 2,169 from 2,369 on fewer walk-ins. Shiro Pacific Place is like for like at 1,371 covers against 1,453 last year, while Shiro Tai Kwun (1,688 covers) has no 2025 records, so its year-on-year reads n/a. Vesu (829 covers) and The Canton Library (682) sit below the portfolio line and are not in these totals; one 180-cover party for 27 November is recorded against both venue records, so it appears once in each of those two rows.
