Read the GA4 bookings below as a floor, not a result. The sevenrooms_booking_complete event is still under-recording across the Hong Kong estate, eleven days after it broke on 15 to 16 October, and it has not been fixed. The clean test is GA4 create-path bookings against SevenRooms website booking rows for the same dates. A healthy venue records at least one GA4 event per website booking, because GA4 counts the booking when it is made and keeps the ones that later cancel. The Canton Library, on the newest property, records 0.93 and is the only venue behaving normally. Every other venue sits between 0.43 and 0.74: Dim Sum Library 0.74, Aqua HK 0.66, Shiro Tai Kwun 0.65, The Chinese Library 0.64, Vesu 0.59, Hutong HK 0.57, Shiro Pacific Place 0.55 and Cantina 0.43. Across the portfolio GA4 recorded 558 bookings against 897 SevenRooms website bookings, so the week is understated by roughly a third. Average session duration is still inflated three to six times at every venue except The Canton Library, which is the same secondary symptom as last week.

Demand went the other way. SevenRooms website bookings rose from 757 to 897, up 18.5 per cent week on week, and portfolio covers reached 10,405, the highest of the eight-week series. Read covers, not bookings, for this week. The covers page carries the detail.

No venue beat the weekly target and none can be read as a trading result.
