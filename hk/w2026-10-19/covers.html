Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

19–25 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 12–18 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 20–26 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. The two measures disagree again this week and covers are the ones to trust. Covers came in at 10,405, up 6.5 per cent week on week and up 35.4 per cent year on year, or up 14.3 per cent like for like once Shiro Tai Kwun, which has no 2025 records, is taken out. It is the highest total of the eight-week series. GA4 create-path bookings fell 5.1 per cent over the same two weeks, which is a measurement problem and not demand: the website booking event has been under-recording across the estate since 15 to 16 October and it is still not fixed. SevenRooms website booking rows for the portfolio rose from 757 to 897 over the same two weeks, up 18.5 per cent.

Website covers are the headline. They rose to 2,705 from 2,327, up 16.2 per cent week on week and 28.9 per cent year on year, and they are the strongest week in the series. Google Reserve rose to 1,037 from 925, up 12.1 per cent. OpenRice eased to 1,808 from 1,976, down 8.5 per cent and the lowest since late August, so the two third-party routes again traded against each other. Bookable covers, website plus OpenRice plus Google Reserve, came to 5,550, up 6.2 per cent week on week and 25.2 per cent year on year.

GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 26 per cent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (17 per cent) and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 47 per cent.

Two things to read with care. Reception covers carry 310 covers of December group business booked inside the week: a 130-cover party at Aqua HK, 100 at Hutong HK and 80 at The Chinese Library, all for December service dates. Strip those three and the portfolio reads 10,095, still up 3.3 per cent week on week, and the bookable total is unaffected. Second, three services closed to the public in the week: Hutong HK on Friday 23 October ran a single 40-cover staff booking against 236 to 410 covers on the six neighbouring days, Cantina on Saturday 24 October was one 206-cover staff booking and nothing else, and The Chinese Library took 6 dinner covers on the 23rd and 3 covers in total on the 24th with no large booking recorded at all. The cause is not visible in the data.

By venue, Hutong HK is the week, 1,928 covers from 1,654 with website covers at 850 from 672. Aqua HK rose to 1,480 from 1,159 and Dim Sum Library to 2,275 from 2,097. Cantina is the only venue down, 605 from 744, and its reception column fell from 308 to 176. The Chinese Library also eased, 935 from 1,107. Vesu (824 covers) and The Canton Library (782, its best week since opening) sit below the portfolio line and are not in these totals.
