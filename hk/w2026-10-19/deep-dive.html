Observations
  • Aqua HK spent HK$6,609 on Meta and Meta recorded 10 schedules, about HK$661 each, against 6 schedules on HK$7,431 the week before. Do not read either number as a CPA. The Meta Schedule conversion fires from the same website confirmation page as the GA4 booking event and both have been under-recording since 15 to 16 October.
  • Cantina spent HK$3,000 and Meta recorded 2 schedules, against 1 the week before. Cantina is still the worst affected venue: GA4 recorded 23 bookings against 53 SevenRooms website bookings, a ratio of 0.43 where a healthy property reads 1.0 or better.
  • Across the eight Hong Kong accounts Meta recorded 45 schedules against 25 the week before, and the rise is almost all The Canton Library (13 to 19), which sits on the newer pixel. Hutong HK recorded a single schedule on HK$4,722 of spend. Treat Meta bookings and Meta CPA for this week as unusable.
