Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

28 Sep–4 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 21–27 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 29 Sep–5 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. This week the two measures point in opposite directions, and covers are the fuller read. GA4 bookings are down 4.7% YoY while covers are up 23.8% YoY, or up 11.1% on a like-for-like basis once Shiro Tai Kwun, which has no 2025 records, is taken out. GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 23 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (25 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 44 percent. Covers came in at 9,873, up 2.4 percent week on week, so demand grew in covers terms even though GA4 bookings fell 6.8 percent. OpenRice had its strongest week of the eight at 2,482 covers, up 20.3 percent week on week, while Google Reserve fell to 784 from 958. That pair of moves is a booking handoff inside the third-party channel, not a change in demand. Hutong HK reception rose to 432 from 129 on two large forward-dated event bookings, 110 covers for 20 November and 89 for 14 February 2027. That is real demand booked early, not a data load. Cantina reception fell back to 171 from 346 after last week’s December event run. Shiro Pacific Place is like for like at 1,918 covers against 1,583 last year, while Shiro Tai Kwun (1,012 covers) has no 2025 records, so its year-on-year reads n/a. Vesu (614 covers) and The Canton Library (411) sit below the portfolio line and are not in these totals.
