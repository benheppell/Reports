Three of six venues beat the weekly target. Cantina (+53.0 percent) and Shiro (+23.8 percent) led it, with The Chinese Library (+22.9 percent) also ahead. The three behind are Dim Sum Library (−23.6 percent), Hutong HK (−23.1 percent) and Aqua HK (−20.9 percent). The shortfall is concentrated in the two largest venues.
