10–16 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 3–9 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 11–17 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong's GA4 bookings are up 14.6% YoY, and covers tell you what that demand is actually worth and where it comes from. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it never sees OpenRice (about a sixth of Hong Kong covers this week), only partly captures Google Reserve, and is completely blind to walk-ins and the reception desks — which between them are close to half of all covers. Read the two together: GA4 is the scoreboard for the channel we control and can optimise, covers are the full demand picture.
