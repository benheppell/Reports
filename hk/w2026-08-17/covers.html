Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

17–23 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 10–16 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 18–24 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong's GA4 bookings are up 12.3% YoY, and covers tell you what that demand is actually worth and where it comes from. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it never sees OpenRice (about a sixth of Hong Kong covers this week), only partly captures Google Reserve, and is completely blind to walk-ins and the reception desks, which between them are close to six tenths of all covers. Read the two together: GA4 is the scoreboard for the channel we control and can optimise, covers are the full demand picture. One caveat on this week's record 13,191 covers: roughly 850 of them come from two one-off private events booked through reception (a 600-cover booking at Aqua Hong Kong and a 250-cover booking at The Chinese Library), so the underlying run rate is closer to 12,300.
