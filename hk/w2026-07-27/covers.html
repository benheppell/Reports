Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

27 Jul–2 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 20–26 Jul 2026 · YoY vs 28 Jul–3 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it never sees OpenRice — around a fifth of Hong Kong covers — only partly captures Google Reserve, and is blind to walk-ins and the reception desks, which together are close to half of all covers created. That is why the two numbers move differently: GA4 reads ▲32.0% YoY on website bookings while total covers created across every channel are +30.8% YoY. Read this as the fuller demand and channel picture — OpenRice strength, heavy walk-in and reception volume at Dim Sum Library and Shiro — not as a contradiction of the GA4 story.
