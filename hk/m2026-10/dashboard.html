SummaryOctober finished at 3,194 bookings against a 4,439 target, -28.0 per cent, 25.6 per cent below September and 28.8 per cent below last October. Read none of that as trade. The website booking event broke on 15 to 16 October, so 17 of October's 31 days sit inside a window where GA4 is recording roughly six bookings in every ten.

The check that settles it does not touch GA4: SevenRooms website booking rows for the same six venues rose from 3,081 in September to 3,575 in October, up 16.0 per cent. September recorded 1.39 GA4 bookings per SevenRooms website booking; October recorded 0.89, which is 64 per cent of September's capture rate. Had October captured at September's rate it would have recorded about 4,984 bookings, ahead of the 4,439 target rather than a quarter short of it. The month is a measurement failure, not a demand failure, and it should not be carried into any trading judgement.

On the numbers as GA4 reports them, all six venues are below target. Dim Sum Library is furthest behind (-36.9 per cent) and Cantina next (-32.1 per cent); Cantina also carries the worst month-on-month fall at -47.4 per cent, having been the estate's worst-affected venue for measurement throughout. Shiro is the least affected against target (-18.9 per cent) and the only venue up on last October (+7.8 per cent), helped by Tai Kwun, which did not exist in October 2025. Aqua HK holds up best month on month at -11.0 per cent.

Monthly figures are on the gross basis, which is what every published month uses, so this October is comparable with September and with last October. Vesu (253 bookings) and The Canton Library (220) are excluded; neither has a target set.
