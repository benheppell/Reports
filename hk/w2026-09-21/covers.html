Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

21–27 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 14–20 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 22–28 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong’s GA4 bookings are up 25.5% YoY and covers are up 60.1% YoY, so the two measures agree on direction and covers show the fuller picture. GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 23 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (22 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 45 percent. Covers came in at 9,853, up 0.4 percent week on week, so demand held flat in covers terms even though GA4 bookings fell 7.1 percent. OpenRice had its strongest week of the eight at 2,185 covers, up 18.2 percent week on week. Cantina covers rose to 959 from 696 almost entirely through its reservations desk, which took several large forward-dated December event bookings, including a single 170-cover party. That is real demand booked early, not a data load. Shiro Pacific Place is like for like at 2,032 covers against 1,134 last year, while Shiro Tai Kwun (1,115 covers) has no 2025 records, so its year-on-year reads n/a. Vesu (486 covers) and The Canton Library (287) sit below the portfolio line and are not in these totals.
