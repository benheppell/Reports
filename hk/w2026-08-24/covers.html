Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

24–30 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 17–23 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 25–31 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong’s GA4 bookings are up 15.8% YoY and covers are up 40.7% YoY, so the two measures agree on direction. Covers add the part GA4 cannot see: GA4 only fires on our own website, so it never counts OpenRice, walk-ins or the reception and reservations desks, which together are close to half of Hong Kong covers. Covers came in at 10,061, down 9.9 percent week on week off an unusually high reception week. Basis note: this report excludes bookings keyed in under individual staff names, which is the documented method. The 3, 10 and 17 August reports wrongly swept those into Reception, so their headline covers totals were overstated and do not line up with the figures here. Week on week and year on year in this report are both rebuilt on the correct basis.
