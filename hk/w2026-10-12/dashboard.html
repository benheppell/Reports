Read this week’s GA4 bookings as a measurement break, not a demand fall. The sevenrooms_booking_complete event stopped recording properly across the Hong Kong estate from 15 to 16 October. At Cantina it recorded zero bookings on 16, 17 and 18 October while the venue still took seven website bookings a day in SevenRooms. Over the same two weeks SevenRooms website bookings for the portfolio rose 4.6 percent, from 746 to 780, while GA4 fell 33 percent. Average session duration jumped three to six times at every venue except The Canton Library, which sits on a newer property and is clean. Google Ads booking conversions at Aqua HK and Hutong HK held flat. Three independent sources say demand was steady, so the figures below understate the week and the variance against target is not a trading result.

On the numbers as GA4 reports them: weekly target = October monthly GA4 target × 7/30, because 12 to 18 October sits wholly inside October, giving a weekly target of 1,036. Hong Kong recorded 588 create-path GA4 bookings, 43.2 percent behind, down 33.1 percent week on week and down 41.8 percent year on year. No venue beat the weekly target. Cantina (−74.6 percent) and Shiro (−55.3 percent against the combined target) show the largest gaps, which is also where the tag break is most visible.

This is the first Hong Kong week on the create-path definition. GA4 bookings now count only the create/checkout and /reservations/<venue> routes and exclude the amendment, manage and pre-arrival pages. The amendment share was 4.1 percent of gross this week and 7.6 percent the week before. In the 2025 comparison week the amendment pages did not exist in the tracking at all, so the 2025 share reads 0 percent and the rule leaves last year unchanged. The prior week and the year-ago week below are both on the create path, so all three columns are on the same basis. The gross figures on the old basis are 613 this week and 951 the week before.
