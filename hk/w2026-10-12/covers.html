Aqua Hong Kong — Covers & Booking Channels

12–18 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 5–11 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 13–19 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. This week the two measures disagree, and covers are the ones to trust. Covers came in at 10,107, up 4.8 percent week on week and up 37.4 percent year on year, or up 17.5 percent like for like once Shiro Tai Kwun, which has no 2025 records, is taken out. GA4 create-path bookings fell 33.1 percent over the same two weeks. The reason is a measurement break, not demand: the website booking event stopped recording properly across the estate from 15 to 16 October, and at Cantina it recorded nothing at all on 16, 17 and 18 October while the venue was still taking seven website bookings a day. Website covers, which come from SevenRooms and not from GA4, rose to 2,427 from 2,287, so the website channel itself grew.

GA4 only fires on our own website, which is 24 percent of Hong Kong covers. It never sees OpenRice (21 percent), and it never sees walk-ins and the reception and reservations desks, which together are 46 percent. OpenRice eased to 2,111 covers from 2,259 after four weeks of growth, while Google Reserve rose to 951 from 900, so the two third-party routes traded against each other this week.

The largest single move in the table is at Shiro and it is a split, not demand: Shiro Pacific Place walk-ins rose to 733 from 447 while Shiro Tai Kwun fell to 847 from 1,023. Added together the two records booked 1,580 walk-in covers against 1,470 the week before. This is the second week running that walk-ins have moved between the two Shiro records, so it is worth asking the Shiro team how the desk is logging them. The Chinese Library rose to 1,154 covers from 840 with reception at 322 against 100, and Hutong HK rose to 1,698 from 1,414 on stronger OpenRice and walk-ins. Aqua HK eased to 1,270 from 1,305. Vesu (751 covers) and The Canton Library (606) sit below the portfolio line and are not in these totals.
