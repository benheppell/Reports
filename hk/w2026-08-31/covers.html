Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Hong Kong’s GA4 bookings are up 13.7% YoY and covers are up 46.2% YoY, so the two measures agree on direction — covers simply show the fuller picture. GA4 only fires on our own website, so it never sees OpenRice (21 percent of covers), walk-ins or the reception and reservations desks, which together are 45 percent of Hong Kong covers. Covers came in at 9,524, down 3.4 percent week on week. Note the Dim Sum Library Google Reserve year-ago figure is a source-data gap (no rows exist for 29 Aug–8 Sep 2025), not a real zero.

Named-host bookings are excluded from this report: large groups and private dining keyed in by the events and reservations teams are not a marketing channel, so they are removed from the covers total rather than parked in an "Other" bucket.
