The Hong Kong booking tag is still broken, but this week it is possible to say exactly how broken, and the answer changes last week's reading. The diagnostic is GA4 create-path bookings divided by SevenRooms website booking rows for the same dates. SevenRooms cannot be affected by anything on our own site, so the ratio isolates measurement from demand. The key correction: a healthy ratio in Hong Kong is not 1.0, it is about 1.17. GA4 counts a booking when it is made, keeps the ones that later cancel and includes the bar, while the SevenRooms figure is net of cancellations and excludes the bar, so GA4 should sit comfortably above SevenRooms. The two clean weeks before the break read 1.14 (W35) and 1.20 (W36). The break then took the portfolio to 0.78 in W37 and 0.64 in W38. This week it is 0.71.

So the ratio improved, and nothing is fixed. Against its own pre-break baseline the portfolio is capturing about 61 per cent of the events it should record. GA4 logged 673 create-path bookings against 946 SevenRooms website bookings; at the pre-break capture rate the week would have read about 1107. Every venue is still short of its own baseline: Dim Sum Library is the least affected at 69 per cent, then The Chinese Library and Aqua HK at 66, Shiro Pacific Place 63, Shiro Tai Kwun 59, and Cantina, Vesu and Hutong HK at 52 to 53 per cent. The Canton Library has stopped being the exception. It read 0.97 last week against a baseline of 1.15 and now reads 0.84, which is 73 per cent capture, so the one property that was working has started to degrade. That is new this week and it is the reason to treat this as unresolved rather than recovering.

Average session duration, the secondary symptom, has come a long way back. It ran three to six times normal last week and is now 357 seconds at Aqua HK and 401 at The Chinese Library against a normal 190 to 220, with Vesu (181) and Shiro (128 to 134) back in range. Published as returned, never substituted.

Demand rose again. SevenRooms website bookings went from 874 to 946, up 8.2 per cent week on week, and portfolio covers reached 10,679, the highest of the eight-week series for the third week running. Read covers, not bookings, for this week. The covers page carries the detail.

On the target. This week straddles the month boundary, six days in October and one in November, so the weekly target is prorated as October monthly ÷ 31 × 6 plus November monthly ÷ 30 × 1, giving 1,003. The method reproduces last week's published target of 1,036 exactly when applied to a week wholly inside October (4,439 × 7 ÷ 30), and it reproduces all six published venue targets exactly, so it is the same basis. Hong Kong recorded 673 create-path GA4 bookings, 32.9 per cent behind, up 20.6 per cent week on week and down 30.9 per cent year on year. No venue beat the weekly target and none of these figures can be read as a trading result.

The year-ago week is a softer base than last week's was: 974 against 1,116 the week before it, which is why the year-on-year gap narrows from 50 to 31 per cent without anything changing in the business. GA4 bookings are the create path only, the third Hong Kong week on that basis; the amendment share of gross was 1.0 per cent this week. Year to date is shown on the old gross basis so the line stays continuous with every published week, and it is 2.2 per cent ahead of target, a figure held down by three broken weeks.
