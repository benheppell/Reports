GA4 bookings = event sevenrooms_booking_complete. Regent St combines Aqua Kyoto and Aqua Nueva.
  • Baseline for each day is the median of that same weekday across the four preceding weeks. Band is baseline plus or minus 20 percent. Red is three or more consecutive days below the band, or a single day under half baseline. Amber is two consecutive days. The run resets the moment a day lands back inside the band.
  • Alerts run on Website covers, not on the raw Booking Widget source. Aqua Shard re-tagged widget traffic into named landing pages on 21 Aug 2026, so the raw widget series has a definition break there. The widget series is carried in the payload but not shown: it measures how a booking was tagged rather than anything actionable.
  • Google conversions and GA4 channel data are deliberately absent: both settle over two to three days and cannot be read on a daily cadence.
  • Covers re-settle downward as cancellations accrue, so the full 35-day window is re-pulled every morning rather than carried forward.
  • Suppressed dates (closures and known buyouts): none.
  • Staff-entered bookings excluded on the reporting day: 358 covers at Hutong, 152 at Aqua Nueva, 150 at Aqua Shard, 36 at Aqua Kyoto, 32 at DSL, 26 at Azzurra, 12 at Shiro. These are events and private dining keyed in by the reservations team, not a marketing channel.
  • Bulk-import check on 2026-09-07: creation times spread across 11 to 22 hours per venue and the largest single booking was 10 covers, so no batch load distorts the day.
  • GA4 bookings fell further than covers on the reporting day (portfolio bookings -29% against baseline, website covers -15%). Either GA4 had not finished processing 7 Sep when this ran, or tracking degraded. Treat the GA4 line as provisional until tomorrow's re-pull confirms it.
  • The Monday baseline for this run includes 31 August 2026, a UK bank holiday and the weakest of the four Mondays in the window, so Monday variances are if anything understated.
