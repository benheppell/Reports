London is framed week on week and year on year rather than against target: the FY26/27 weekly targets were set before the Shard’s traffic shift and the Dim Sum Library rebrand, so variance to them is not a clean read, and DSL is flagged not comparable for the same reason. London booked 3,087 GA4 bookings, down 3.4 percent week on week and down 4.6 percent year on year, against a weekly target of 3,733 carried here for reference only. The year-on-year gap is again Aqua Shard, which is 427 bookings below last year, almost three times the whole London shortfall of 148; the other five venues together are up 18.7 percent year on year. Aqua Shard itself rose 4.5 percent week on week to 1,315, its second best week since early September, and it did that while Tuesday 6 October was closed to the public for an exclusive hire. Hutong is the week’s swing, down 15.7 percent week on week from a restated 1,032 and 6.1 percent below last year, on traffic 12.2 percent below last year. Regent St was level week on week at 207 but is 34.7 percent below last year on traffic 42.0 percent below. Shiro Sushi is the only venue ahead on both measures, up 19.6 percent week on week and 2.7 percent year on year. Conversion still reads better than traffic: it is above last year at Hutong (5.2 against 4.9 percent), Regent St (4.9 against 4.4), Azzurra (5.1 against 4.9) and Shiro Sushi (8.7 against 5.2), and down only at Aqua Shard, 4.9 percent against 6.3 percent.
