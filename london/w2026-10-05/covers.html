Aqua London — Covers & Booking Channels

5–11 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 6–12 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. London’s GA4 bookings are down 4.6% YoY while covers are up 37.4% YoY, and both are right. GA4’s sevenrooms_booking_complete only fires for website bookings, so OpenTable is invisible to it and Google Reserve is only partly captured. Website is 60 percent of London covers; the other 40 percent, made up of OpenTable (12 percent), Google Reserve (8 percent), walk-ins (15 percent) and the rest, never reaches the GA4 number. Covers came in at 11,502, down 2.2 percent week on week against a freshly recomputed prior week, and it is the second highest of the eight weeks on record. Aqua Shard is not like for like this week: Tuesday 6 October was taken as an exclusive hire of the whole day, which closed the restaurant to the public and is worth about 366 covers against the matched Tuesday last year. Read the GA4 dashboard as the website channel and this report as demand.
