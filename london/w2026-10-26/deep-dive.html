Observations
  • Meta CPAs: Azzurra £13, Hutong £13, Aqua Shard £13, Shiro Sushi £17, Regent St £21. Three of the five deep-dive venues are now inside the £15 good band, where only one was last week. DSL is read as spend to traffic to bookings rather than a cost per booking.
  • Meta spend fell hard across London: £7,925 against £12,341, down 36 per cent, the lowest of the eight weeks. Schedules fell only 16 per cent, 537 against 636, so portfolio CPA improved from £19.40 to £14.76. The cuts are not even: Hutong £2,112 to £1,094, Shiro Sushi £1,114 to £399, DSL £3,711 to £1,577 and Regent St £1,214 to £576 are all roughly halved or worse, while Aqua Shard rose to £2,990 and Azzurra held at £1,289. It splits two ways, a prospecting campaign at £842 for 72 and a jazz night campaign at £426 for 31. Hutong went the other way: spend halved and schedules fell 38 per cent, 133 to 83, which is a worse than proportional loss and the only venue where the cut clearly cost volume.

Observations
  • Read the Aqua Shard and Hutong rows in the Google table below as raw action counts, not as published figures. Both venues' Google booking conversions are withheld from the headline, from ad-driven bookings, from blended CPA and from the paid funnel on the ads report. They are shown here because this is the diagnostic page and the numbers are the evidence. Do not read a cost per booking from either row.
  • Aqua Shard and Hutong Google booking conversions are both published as gaps again, and the reason has changed at Aqua Shard. The WEBPAGE action “Booking Complete Sevenrooms” recorded all seven days this week for a total of 318.46, back inside its W32 to W36 range of 311 to 417, while the GA4-imported action also ran all seven days at 89.39. So the no-ga4 rule now produces a complete, in-range, seven-day figure and the original reason for the gap has gone. What remains is the double count if the two were ever summed, which the rule already prevents. This needs a decision rather than another week of withholding. At Hutong the picture is different: the page tag recorded on six of seven days at 19 to 29 a day and nothing on 1 November, for a week total of 151.39 against a September baseline near 39 a day. The wild intermittency of W37 and W38 has gone but the under-recording has not, so that gap stands. Raw values for both are in _raw_ads.json.
  • Where Google is published it reads well. Shiro Sushi delivered about 43 booking conversions on £511, roughly £12 each, its best of the eight weeks and inside the £18 good band. DSL delivered 33 on £647, £20 each. Azzurra delivered 10 on £312, £31 each, just past the poor threshold on small volume.
  • Regent St is the one to look at. Google spend rose again, £1,098 to £1,672, up 52 per cent on top of last week's doubling, so it has tripled in a fortnight from £532. That bought 14 booking conversions against 6, so CPA improved from £183 to £119, but it is still four times the poor threshold. Meanwhile its Meta spend was halved. Money has moved from Meta to Google at this venue and the Google CPA does not yet justify it.

  • Users against last year at the deep-dive venues: Aqua Shard -7.5 per cent, Hutong +16.3 per cent, Regent St -37.7 per cent, Azzurra -34.9 per cent, Shiro Sushi -52.8 per cent. The prior week was re-pulled and is unchanged at 780.5, so last week's corrupted figure did not restate: the corruption ended rather than settling.   • Aqua Shard is the only venue where traffic, collection and conversion all hold together: users 29,278, the highest of the eight weeks, create-path bookings 1,219 and a collection ratio of 1.01. Its year-on-year booking fall of 25.6 per cent sits against a users fall of only 7.5 per cent, and conversion of 4.2 per cent against 5.2 per cent last year, so this is a conversion story at Aqua Shard rather than a measurement one. That is the opposite of the other four and it is the more serious finding.
