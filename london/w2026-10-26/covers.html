Aqua London — Covers & Booking Channels

26 Oct–1 Nov 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 19–25 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 27 Oct–2 Nov 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Covers came in at 12,191, up 6.3 per cent week on week against a freshly recomputed prior week of 11,469, and the highest of the eight weeks on record. Website is 56 per cent of London covers; OpenTable, Google Reserve, walk-ins and the rest never reach the GA4 number. Year on year the portfolio reads up 56 per cent, but DSL was closed for its rebrand in the matched 2025 week and contributes 3,331 covers against nothing last year: ex DSL the figure is up 13.7 per cent. Bookable covers, meaning website plus OpenTable plus Google Reserve, were 9,333, up 4.2 per cent on the week and up 10.1 per cent on last year ex DSL. The bulk-import rule removed 105 covers from 39 OpenTable bookings, 82 covers at DSL, 20 at Azzurra and 3 at Aqua Shard. Read the GA4 dashboard as the website channel and this report as demand: four of the six GA4 properties under-recorded all week while covers rose.

Covers = SevenRooms covers by booking-created date (sr_created_at, Europe/London), net of cancellations and deleted bookings. Bar seating areas excluded; private dining and afternoon tea included. Named-host bookings are excluded from this report: large groups and private dining keyed in by the events and reservations teams are not a marketing channel, so they are removed from the covers total rather than parked in an "Other" bucket.
