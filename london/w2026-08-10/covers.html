Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website widget, so it is blind to OpenTable, only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reception and host desks. That is why the GA4 dashboard reads -10.7% YoY while total covers are materially ahead of last year. The GA4 number is the right measure of the channel we control; covers are the right measure of the business.
