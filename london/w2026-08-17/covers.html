17–23 Aug 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 10–16 Aug 2026 · YoY vs 18–24 Aug 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 only fires for bookings made on our own website, so it is blind to OpenTable, only partly captures Google Reserve, and never sees walk-ins or the reception and host desks. That is why the GA4 dashboard reads -9.4% YoY while total covers are 13,665 against 9,937 the same week last year. The GA4 number is the right measure of the channel we control; covers are the right measure of the business. Worth watching: OpenTable covers are at an eight-week low for the second week running, concentrated in Aqua Shard and Regent St.
