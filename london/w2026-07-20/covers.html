20–26 Jul 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 13–19 Jul 2026 · YoY vs 21–27 Jul 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. GA4 is becoming steadily less reliable as cookie consent and privacy tracking cut what it can see, so treat it as a guide to overall website performance rather than a count of bookings. GA4 sevenrooms_booking_complete only fires for bookings made on our own website widget, so OpenTable is invisible to GA4 and Google Reserve is only partly captured; walk-ins and reception desks are never seen. OpenTable covers are 1,672 this week, up from 386 the same week last year, roughly 4.3x. So while GA4 reads ▼4.3% YoY, total covers created are +35% YoY — covers run well ahead of the GA4 trend, and this report is the more accurate view of the demand we are actually creating.

Covers = SevenRooms covers by booking-created date (sr_created_at, Europe/London), net of cancellations and deleted bookings. Bar seating areas excluded; private dining and afternoon tea included.
