19–25 Oct 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 12–18 Oct 2026 · YoY vs 20–26 Oct 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. Covers came in at 11,742, down 4.1 percent week on week against a freshly recomputed prior week of 12,243, and the second highest of the eight weeks on record. Website is 58 percent of London covers; OpenTable, Google Reserve, walk-ins and the rest never reach the GA4 number. Year on year the portfolio reads up 51 percent, but DSL was closed for its rebrand in the matched 2025 week and contributes 3,239 covers against nothing last year: ex DSL the figure is up 9.1 percent. The bulk-import rule removed 14 covers from 7 OpenTable bookings, 8 covers at Hutong and 6 at Azzurra. Read the GA4 dashboard as the website channel, and read this report as demand: four of the six GA4 properties under-recorded all week while covers held.
