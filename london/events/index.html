Enquiries: Tripleseat leads created in the period (London time). Conversion: share of those leads whose event has since been confirmed. Confirmed: events whose booking was marked Definite in the month, whatever has happened to them since. Held: Closed or Definite events dated in the period. Value: the actual amount, else the grand total, else the F&B minimum, divided by 1.2. Financial year runs February to January. Forecast: confirmed upcoming events valued at their F&B minimum times the venue's uplift last year (actual spend ÷ F&B minimum on closed events), never below their current value. Booking type comes from the room map; events in rooms not yet mapped show as Unmapped.
