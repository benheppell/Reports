Enquiries: Tripleseat leads created in the period (London time). Conversion: share of those leads whose event has since been confirmed. Confirmed: events whose booking was marked Definite in the month, whatever has happened to them since. Held: Closed or Definite events dated in the period. Value: the actual amount, else the grand total, else the F&B minimum, divided by 1.2. Financial year runs February to January. Confirmed upcoming: events after the month end that were already confirmed by then (for a past month, ones that have since happened count at what they actually spent). Forecast: confirmed upcoming events valued at their F&B minimum times the venue's uplift last year (actual spend ÷ F&B minimum on closed events), never below their current value. Booking type comes from the room map; events in rooms not yet mapped show as Unmapped. Weekly view: Monday to Sunday, against the same week last year (364 days earlier). Lost: events first marked Lost in the period, by the reason recorded in Tripleseat.
