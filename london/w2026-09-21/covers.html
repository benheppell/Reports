21–27 Sep 2026 (Mon–Sun) · SevenRooms covers by booking-created date · WoW vs 14–20 Sep 2026 · YoY vs 22–28 Sep 2025
Why this sits alongside the GA4 dashboard. London’s GA4 bookings are down 6.0% YoY while covers are up 29.5% YoY, and both are right. GA4’s sevenrooms_booking_complete only fires for website bookings, so OpenTable is invisible to it and Google Reserve is only partly captured. Website is 56 percent of London covers; the other 44 percent, made up of OpenTable (11 percent), Google Reserve (10 percent), walk-ins (16 percent) and the rest, never reaches the GA4 number. Covers came in at 11,696, up 1.1 percent week on week against a freshly recomputed prior week. Read the GA4 dashboard as the website channel and this report as demand.

Covers = SevenRooms covers by booking-created date (sr_created_at, Europe/London), net of cancellations and deleted bookings. Bar seating areas excluded; private dining and afternoon tea included. Website = all bookings originating on our own site: booking widget, campaign landing pages, on-site CTAs (Nav/Hero), menu page and paid-search landing pages. OpenTable = OpenTable and OT Guestcenter sources; Google Reserve = Google Reserve Integration; Walk-in = Walk In; Reception = venue reception and reservations desks; TheFork / First Table and Other 3rd party = booking platforms, gifting and concierge partners. Named-host bookings are excluded from this report: large groups and private dining keyed in by the events and reservations teams are not a marketing channel, so they are removed from the covers total rather than parked in an "Other" bucket. Covers totals here are therefore lower than in reports before 13 Jul 2026 and are not comparable with them. Regent St = Aqua Kyoto + Aqua Nueva combined.
