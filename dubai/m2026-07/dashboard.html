July 2026 (full calendar month) · AED · MoM vs June 2026 · YoY vs July 2025 · one venue
The read. July held the line — and beat the summer forecast. Completed covers were 2,845, essentially flat on June (+0.5%) and well above the ~2,050 the seasonal model had projected for July, so the deep summer trough hasn't materialised. Completed revenue rose 4% MoM to AED 944k on a stronger check (AED 332/cover, +10% YoY) — fewer guests than last summer but each spending more. The year-on-year gap (covers −18%, revenue −10%, web bookings −29%) is still a footfall/website story against a strong July 2025, cushioned by the reservations team, walk-ins and paid media. Paid spent AED 29.6k at a blended AED 85 per booking (better than June's 93). Meta is now vastly more efficient than last July (CPA AED 91 vs 169) and Google re-introduced P.Max — but Meta remains a one-product, daytime story, with no support behind dinner / à la carte.

7 · What's working / what's not

Working

  • Covers held flat MoM and beat the summer forecast — footfall more resilient than projected.
  • Revenue up 4% MoM on a stronger check (AED 332/cover, +10% YoY).
  • Cancellations down to 9.7% — cleaner, higher-quality book.
  • Meta efficiency transformed YoY: CPA AED 91 vs 168, CPM AED 8 vs 27; blended paid CPA improved to AED 85.
  • Google brand at AED 56/booking on a 30% CTR — the single most efficient line.

Not working / watch

  • Website is the real weak spot: users −17% YoY, web bookings −29%, conversion easing.
  • Meta is 100% daytime/brunch — no support for dinner, à la carte, Dark Brunch or Red Lantern.
  • Imperial Brunch = 55% of Meta spend; heavy single-product concentration.
  • New P.Max > Generic running at AED 161 CPA — needs a few weeks to prove out.
  • Net created covers −9% MoM — keep an eye on forward demand as summer ends.
