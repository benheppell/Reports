August 2026 (full calendar month) · AED · MoM vs July 2026 · YoY vs August 2025 · one venue
The read. August was the summer low — completed covers of 2,635, down 7.4% on July and 16% on last August, as the seasonal trough finally arrived. Revenue fell 11% MoM to AED 844k. But the forward book turned sharply: net created covers jumped 25% MoM to 3,766 (and +10% YoY), most of it bookings made in August for September dates — the recovery is loading. Website users actually rose (+8% MoM as paid pushed more traffic) but conversion slipped to 6.5%, the softest of the summer and the clear watch item. On paid, Meta softened with the market (bookings 173→133, CPA up to AED 119) while Google improved (CPA down to AED 70, P.Max scaling); blended CPA AED 90. Meta is still a daytime/brunch-only story, with nothing behind dinner or à la carte.

6 · Forward book & outlook

The forward signalDubai books almost same-day, so the forward book is normally empty — which makes this month's 25% jump in net created covers (3,766) notable: it's well above what dined in August, so a real block of September service dates is already on the books. That points to the autumn rebound arriving on cue.

August was the seasonal floor; September historically recovers strongly, and this year the forward book is already loading for it. Expect covers to climb back through September.
PlanRide the September recovery. On media: keep Google's efficient brand + P.Max running, and open a dinner / à la carte line on Meta (the biggest coverage gap — pending the Eye Studio video). On the website — soft all summer — the migration is the priority for lifting on-site conversion.

7 · What's working / what's not

Working

  • Forward book jumped 25% MoM — September rebound is loading.
  • Cancellations down again to 7.4% of gross created — a clean book.
  • Google improved into the trough: CPA down to AED 70, brand at AED 42/booking.
  • Revenue per cover still up 5% YoY despite the seasonal dip.
  • Meta far ahead of last August (CPA AED 119 vs 203).

Not working / watch

  • Covers at the summer floor — down 16% YoY, revenue −12% YoY.
  • Website conversion slipped to 6.5% despite more traffic — migration should fix this.
  • Meta CPA jumped to AED 119 as August demand thinned.
  • Meta still 100% daytime — no dinner, à la carte, Dark Brunch or Red Lantern support.
  • P.Max > Generic still the pricier Google line (AED 135) — keep watching.
